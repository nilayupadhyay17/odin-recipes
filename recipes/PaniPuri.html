Pani Puri

Home

Description

Pani puri is a popular street food in India, consisting of hollow, crispy puris filled with a mixture of flavored water (pani), tamarind chutney, chili, chaat masala, potato, onion, and chickpeas.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Semolina (Sooji)
  • 1/4 cup All-purpose flour (Maida)
  • 1/4 tsp Baking soda
  • Water (as needed)
  • Oil (for deep frying)
  • 1 cup Boiled potatoes (mashed)
  • 1/2 cup Boiled chickpeas
  • 1/2 cup Tamarind chutney
  • 1/2 cup Mint-coriander chutney
  • 1 tsp Chaat masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 tsp Red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp Roasted cumin powder

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix semolina, all-purpose flour, and baking soda.
  2. Add water gradually and knead to form a smooth dough.
  3. Cover the dough and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Roll out small portions of the dough into thin circles.
  5. Heat oil in a deep frying pan and fry the puris until golden brown.
  6. Drain on paper towels and let them cool.
  7. Prepare the filling by mixing boiled potatoes, chickpeas, chaat masala, salt, red chili powder, and roasted cumin powder.
  8. To serve, make a small hole in each puri, fill it with the potato-chickpea mixture, add tamarind and mint-coriander chutneys, and pour flavored water (pani) over it.
