Description

biryani is a mixed rice dish originating among the Muslims of the Indian subcontinent as a variety of Persian pilaf. It is made with Indian spices, rice, and usually some type of meat (chicken, beef, goat, lamb, prawn, or fish), and sometimes, in certain varieties, eggs or vegetables such as potatoes in certain regional varieties.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Basmati rice
  • 1/2 cup Chicken (cut into pieces)
  • 1/4 cup Yogurt
  • 1 Onion (sliced)
  • 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tsp Ginger (grated)
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp Red Chili Powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups Water
  • 2 tbsp Oil or Ghee

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. In a large pot, heat oil or ghee over medium heat.
  3. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  4. Add minced garlic and grated ginger, sauté for 1-2 minutes.
  5. Add chicken pieces and cook until they are no longer pink.
  6. Stir in yogurt, garam masala, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt. Cook for 5 minutes.
  7. Add rinsed rice and water. Bring to a boil.
  8. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes or until the rice is cooked and water is absorbed.
  9. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve hot.
